Understanding the 11+1 Gold Scheme: How Bonus Months Maximize Your Wealth

For generations, Indian families have relied on physical gold not merely as ceremonial adornment, but as the premier tangible hedge against inflation and economic volatility. However, purchasing lump sum gold during price peaks can strain family finances. This is where systematic gold accumulation—specifically Shalom Gold’s 11+1 Smart Gold Plan—has transformed the gold savings paradigm.

Under the 11+1 plan, you choose a comfortable monthly installment starting from just ₹1,000 up to ₹1,00,000. You deposit 11 equal monthly installments. On the 12th month, Shalom Gold funds the full 12th installment directly into your account as a scheme bonus. This translates to an immediate effective yield of 8.33% to 10% on your contributed capital, paid in pure gold value.

"Gold is money. Everything else is credit. Disciplined monthly gold accumulation backed by guaranteed bonus contributions is the smartest way to build family heirlooms."

Upon maturity on the 12th month, investors have complete flexibility to redeem their accumulated value in 100% physical BIS hallmarked gold:

  • 24K Minted Gold Bars: 999.9 pure cast bullion bars with assay certification and unique serial numbers.
  • Swiss CertiCard Coins: 1g, 5g, 10g, or 50g Lakshmi/Ganesha minted coins with 0% making charges.
  • Handcrafted 22K Jewellery: Exclusive making charge waivers on signature bridal and temple collections.

Every gram of gold allocated is stored in insured tier-IV vaults with legal bailment agreements and updated in real-time to your digital passbook. Start securing your family milestone gold today.
