Each Shalom Gold jewellery piece is forged with generational devotion by master goldsmiths across traditional jewellery hubs of Mumbai, Surat, Rajkot, and Jaipur. Using authentic die-casting, hand filigree, and antique brush finishes, our ornaments bridge timeless cultural legacy with modern certified standards.

Every item is delivered in a luxurious velvet-lined jewel box along with a physical BIS Hallmark Verification Card and a certified invoice with GST breakdown.

Under Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) regulations, 100% of Shalom Gold inventory is hallmarked with a unique 6-digit alphanumeric HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification). Customers can verify the authenticity, assaying centre, and purity of their piece at any time via the official BIS Care Mobile App.

Shalom Gold guarantees an honest, zero-loss buyback on all physical gold and bullion sold. Exchange or liquidate your jewellery at prevailing daily IBJA benchmark gold rates at any of our authorized retail stores across India with transparent weighing and instant bank disbursement.

To preserve the lustrous glow of your gold ornaments, store individually in airtight zip-lock pouches within the provided velvet casing. Keep away from harsh perfumes, chlorine, and chemical cleaners. Complimentary ultrasonic cleaning is provided for lifetime at all Shalom Gold retail branches.
