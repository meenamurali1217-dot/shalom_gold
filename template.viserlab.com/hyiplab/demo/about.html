Our Heritage & Commitment

Founded by Master Goldsmith Shri Ramdas Zaveri in Zaveri Bazaar, Mumbai, Shalom Gold began with an unwavering dedication to pure hallmarked gold and honest artisan craftsmanship.

Over four decades, Shalom Gold pioneered transparent Karatmeter purity testing, introduced Swiss-grade minted CertiCard bullion coins, and expanded to 12 flagship experiential lounges across India. Today, we empower over 500,000 families to save in pure physical gold with a zero-deduction lifetime buyback guarantee and asset-backed franchise opportunities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our gold purity certifications, monthly bonus schemes, and security.

You contribute your chosen monthly installment (e.g. ₹5,000) for 11 consecutive months. On the 12th month, Shalom Gold funds the full 12th installment as your bonus. You then redeem the entire maturity amount in 100% physical 24K gold coins, minted bars, or 22K hallmarked jewellery with 0% making charges on coins.

100% of accumulated gold is held in bank-grade, fully insured tier-IV vaults under individual custody bailment agreements. Your assets are fully audited and protected with zero counterparty risk.

We guarantee a lifetime buyback at 100% of the gold weight at prevailing market rates without arbitrary melting losses or hidden commissions, setting the industry benchmark for honesty.

Entrepreneurs with an investment capacity of ₹50 Lakhs+ can apply to open a turnkey Shalom Gold luxury showroom. The capital includes ₹30 Lakhs in physical gold inventory and ₹20 Lakhs as a refundable security deposit, generating steady retail spreads and recurring monthly SIP commissions.
